Common Questions

Song not being recognised?

  • Make sure the music is playing clearly and your phone mic isn't covered.
  • Try again in a quieter environment.
  • SongBru works best with commercially released songs.

Lyrics not syncing correctly?

  • Tap any lyric line to re-sync from that point.
  • You can also scroll the lyrics — the view will sync to wherever you release.

Subscription / billing issues?

  • Use the "Restore purchases" button on the paywall screen.
  • Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID — visit Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to manage or cancel.

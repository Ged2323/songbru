🎵 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

Audio: When you tap the mic, a short audio fingerprint is sent to AudD (audd.io) to identify the song. This audio is not stored by us and is processed in real time only.

Purchase data: Subscription purchases are processed through Apple and managed via RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of subscription status linked to your device ID. We do not store payment information.

Device ID: Used by RevenueCat to manage your subscription entitlement across app restarts.

Local data: Sync offset preferences and free search count are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any personal identifiers. We do not sell data to third parties. We do not use data for advertising.

Third-party services

AudD — song identification. See their privacy policy at audd.io.

RevenueCat — subscription management. See their privacy policy at revenuecat.com.

lrclib.net — synced lyrics. No personal data is sent; only song title and artist name.
